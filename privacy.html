About M7 Tech Academy

M7 Tech Academy is a free learning portal run by Stanley Bagambisa under Team Yoweri 24/7. Our mission is to train world-class scientists, engineers and technicians who convert African problems into opportunities — creators and distributors of opportunities, not seekers and hunters of them.

The site offers 8 free tech career roadmaps (coding, AI, web, mobile, data, cloud, cybersecurity, freelancing) with 100+ curated free resources, checkpoint quizzes and on-device progress tracking. No account is required and all lessons are free forever.

Contact: message us via the Team Yoweri 24/7 Facebook group.

Privacy Policy

1. DATA WE STORE

Your learning progress (completed skills, resources, quiz passes, bookmarks) is stored only in your own browser's localStorage on your device. We operate no accounts, no servers and no database — we never see, collect or transmit your progress.

2. ADVERTISING COOKIES (ADSTERRA & FUTURE GOOGLE ADSENSE)

We currently show banner ads from Adsterra, which may use cookies and similar technologies to serve, frequency-cap and measure ads. Learn how Adsterra handles data at adsterra.com/privacy-policy. If we switch to Google AdSense later, Google and its partners may use cookies for personalised advertising — you can opt out at google.com/settings/ads and learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. THIRD-PARTY RESOURCES

Roadmaps link to external learning sites (freeCodeCamp, Kaggle, Hugging Face, MDN and others). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Embedded fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

4. CHILDREN

This site is a general learning resource suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

5. YOUR CONTROL

Clear your browser's site data at any time to erase all stored progress. Blocking cookies may disable future advertising but never blocks learning content.

6. CHANGES & CONTACT

Material changes will be noted here with a new effective date. Questions: reach us via the Facebook group.
